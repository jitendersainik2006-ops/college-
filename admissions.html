FAQ

Admissions questions families ask most.

When do admissions open?

Admissions for the 2026 academic year are open now, with assessment slots released in phases.

Are scholarships available?

Yes. Merit, sports, arts, and need-aware scholarships are reviewed after assessment and counseling.

Can we visit the campus?

Yes. Families can book a guided campus tour through the contact page or admissions office.
