Our mission

"To teach students to educate themselves."

Smt. Indramani Mandelia Shiksha Niket is affiliated to Pandit Deendayal Upadhyaya Shekhawati University, Sikar, and serves learners in Pilani with English-medium instruction, secure infrastructure, laboratories, library access, transport, and hostel facilities.
