About us

A values-led P.G. college with a modern academic heartbeat.

IMSN serves Pilani with English-medium higher education, secure infrastructure, practical laboratories, and a culture of self-learning.

Our story

Built for inquiry, character, and opportunity.

IMSN is a co-educational P.G. college affiliated to Pandit Deendayal Upadhyaya Shekhawati University, Sikar. The institution supports higher education in science, commerce, arts, computer application, and postgraduate studies.


Campus life includes NCC, NSS, Rover & Ranger, sports, library, hostel, practical labs, transport, and student activities.
